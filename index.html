THE HOUSE OF KG

Born on the looms of Ahmedabad

KG Traders is a manufacturing house — not a storefront. Every silhouette that carries the KG monogram is designed, cut, embroidered and finished under one roof in Ahmedabad, then shipped in wholesale volumes to retailers across India.

From hand-worked zari crop tops to double-layered tissue shararas, our collections are built for the boutique rack: consistent sizing, repeatable quality, and design languages that sell through the season.
